What do you need to start?

A short brief, the pages you need, your content and branding assets, and any required integrations. We confirm the deliverables and timeline before work begins.

Can you build an e-commerce website?

We can scope storefront pages, products and a cart interface. Real payments, order processing, stock and delivery services need their own implementation and testing; a visual storefront alone is not a complete operational store.

Are these paid client projects?

They are presented as personal development projects and prototypes. No paid-client claim, performance result or testimonial is implied.
